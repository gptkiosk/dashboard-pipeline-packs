# upubhub class codes

A class code reads `SUBJ:LL-Class name`, for example `TECH:10-How LLMs Are Trained`. Copy it from the project settings.

- SUBJ: 3 to 5 capital letters. Pick the most specific code that fits; use GEN when nothing fits.
- LL: reading level as a grade equivalent in two digits: 00 kindergarten, 01 to 12 grades, 13 to 16 college years. General adult audiences read well at 09 or 10.
- Files and links use the slug: lowercase, dashes, for example `tech-10-how-llms-are-trained`.

Subject codes:

- GEN: general knowledge: the catch-all for everyday topics that fit no single subject
- NEWS: current events and how today's things work, for example what neoclouds are
- TECH: technology and computing in everyday life, for example how LLMs are trained
- ENGR: engineering and how machines work, for example jet engines
- COMP: computer science and programming
- SCI: general science
- PHYS: physics
- CHEM: chemistry
- BIO: biology
- ASTR: astronomy and space
- ENVS: environment and climate
- HEAL: health and the body
- MATH: mathematics
- FIN: personal finance and money
- ECON: economics
- BUS: business
- LAW: law and rights
- CIV: civics and government
- HIST: history
- GEOG: geography
- SOC: society and sociology
- PSYC: psychology
- PHIL: philosophy and ethics
- ENGL: English language and writing
- LIT: literature
- LANG: world languages
- MEDIA: media, film and journalism
- ART: visual art and design
- MUSC: music
