# Lesson plan design

- Objectives a teacher can check: "explain", "compare", "solve", "give an example of". Two or three per lesson.
- Warm-up connects to what students already know; the main activity does the teaching; the check for understanding tests the objectives, not trivia.
- Every activity says what the teacher does, what students do, and how long it takes. Leave a few minutes of slack.
- Match the approach setting: discussion plans name the questions, hands-on plans list every material, reading and writing plans name the reading.
- When students struggle: the likely misunderstanding and one concrete way to address it.
- State facts you are sure of. Mark anything uncertain for the fact-check story instead of guessing.
- Provided text (content source provided) is the teacher's. Copy it as written; report gaps to the teacher instead of filling them.
